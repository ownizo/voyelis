## What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage, work in the same way and are treated here as cookies.

## Which categories we use

| Category | What it is for | Consent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Strictly necessary** | Storing your cookie choices (local storage `vy-consent`, 12 months) and your language choice (`vy-lang`), and keeping the site working and secure. | Not required |
| **Statistics** | A random session identifier (session storage `vy-sid`, deleted when you close the tab) to group the pages viewed in one visit in our statistics. | Only with your consent |
| **Marketing** | Measuring campaigns and showing relevant advertising on other platforms. | Only with your consent |

If we use **Google Tag Manager** (Google Ireland Ltd.) to manage measurement tags, it runs in "consent mode": until you accept the "Statistics" or "Marketing" categories, Google tools do not store cookies on your device; once you accept, Google Analytics cookies (`_ga`, `_ga_*`, up to 2 years) and, with "Marketing", Google campaign measurement cookies may be used. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

## Cookie-free visit counter

To know how many people visit the site and where they come from, each page viewed sends our internal system the page address, the referring page or site (including campaign parameters) and the time of the visit, and, when you leave, the time spent on the page. The server adds the country and the device type (computer, phone or tablet). We do not store your IP address or browser identification, and this counter stores nothing on your device. Search engine robots are excluded.

## Third-party content and sites

Offer icons are loaded from HelloSafe servers, which receive your IP address in order to deliver them. When you choose an offer and move to the HelloSafe platform, the HelloSafe cookies and cookie policy apply.

## How to manage cookies

You can accept, refuse or customise cookies in the banner shown on your first visit and change your choice at any time with the button above or the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings.
