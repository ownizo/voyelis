## 1. Who we are

The website voyelis.com ("Voyelis") is run by **Ownizo, Unipessoal Lda.**, Av. do Atlântico 16, Esc. 5.07, 1990-019 Lisboa, Portugal ("we"). We are the controller of the data collected on this site. Privacy contact: [info@voyelis.com](mailto:info@voyelis.com).

Voyelis is a digital marketing and information platform for travel insurance. **We do not sell insurance or conclude insurance contracts on this site.** Purchase, payment and policy management are carried out by HELLO SAFE SAS ("HelloSafe"), which processes the data you give it on its platform as an independent controller, under its own privacy policy.

## 2. What data we process

### a) Insurance comparison

To show you offers, the comparison form only asks for **trip details**: country of residence, destination countries, dates, type of trip, the age of each traveller and, if you choose, the total trip cost, the booking date and whether you will do adventure sports. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, address or health data.

### b) Technical data

Like any website, our hosting provider records technical data needed for operation and security (IP address, browser type, pages requested, date and time), kept for short periods for troubleshooting and abuse prevention.

### c) Visit statistics

Our own cookie-free counter records each page viewed: page address, source of the visit (referring site and campaign parameters), country, device type and time on page. It does not store your IP address or browser identification. If you accept the "Statistics" category, a random session identifier is also used to group the pages of one visit. This data is stored in the internal database of the Ownizo group, hosted in the European Union (Supabase, Ireland), and kept for up to 25 months.

### d) Email contact

If you write to us, we process the data you send (name, email and message) in order to reply.

### e) Cookies and local storage

See the [cookie policy](/cookies).

## 4. Who we share data with

- **HelloSafe** (HELLO SAFE SAS, RCS Rennes 883 069 593, 5 allée de la Grande Treille, 35200 Rennes, France): receives the trip details to calculate prices and, if you choose an offer, to prepare the purchase. From then on, the data you enter on the HelloSafe platform (identity, contact details, payment, any health data) is processed exclusively by HelloSafe and the insurers under their own privacy policies. HelloSafe informs us of the sales and cancellations linked to our reference (sale identifier, insurer, amounts and status), without the traveller's personal data.
- **Service providers** acting on our behalf (processors): website hosting and server functions (Netlify), visit statistics database (Supabase), tag management and statistics with consent (Google Tag Manager / Google Analytics, Google Ireland Ltd., only if enabled) and service emails (Resend).
- **Authorities**, where required by law.

We do not sell personal data.

## 5. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Some service providers may process data outside the EEA, in particular in the United States. In those cases, transfers rely on an adequacy decision of the European Commission (including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, where the provider is certified) or on standard contractual clauses approved by the Commission.

## 6. How long we keep data

- Comparison data (trip details and reference): up to 24 months, to attribute and reconcile sales and commissions with HelloSafe.
- Sales and cancellation records reported by HelloSafe: for the legal retention period for accounting records (10 years).
- Emails: for as long as needed to deal with the matter and then up to 3 years.
- Hosting technical logs: for the provider's periods, usually short.

## 7. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, object to processing based on legitimate interest and withdraw any consent at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing), by writing to [info@voyelis.com](mailto:info@voyelis.com). For data processed on the HelloSafe platform, contact HelloSafe. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, the [CNPD](https://www.cnpd.pt), or with the supervisory authority of the country where you live.

## 8. Security

The site uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Our access credentials to the HelloSafe platform are kept on the server only and are never exposed in the browser.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. The version in force is always the one published on this page, with the date of the last update.
