You book months ahead, pay a deposit, buy flights — and then life happens. Trip cancellation insurance exists for that moment: it refunds what you cannot get back from airlines, hotels and tour operators when you have to cancel for a covered reason.

## What it refunds

The non-refundable costs of your trip, up to the amount insured:

- flights and train tickets,
- accommodation and holiday rentals,
- package holidays, cruises and tours,
- deposits and cancellation fees charged by the provider.

Taxes and charges that airlines must refund anyway (such as some airport taxes) are generally not covered — ask the airline first.

## Covered reasons

Policies follow one of two logics.

**Listed reasons.** The conditions name each covered event. Typical examples:

- serious illness, accident or death of the insured person, a travelling companion or a close relative;
- complications of pregnancy;
- serious damage to your home or business (fire, flood, burglary);
- redundancy or a new job;
- summons to court or jury service;
- theft of identity documents shortly before departure.

**Any justified reason.** Broader policies cover any unforeseeable event beyond your control that you can document, sometimes with a higher excess.

Never covered: a reason that existed or was foreseeable when you bought the policy, and a simple change of mind.

## When to buy it

**As soon as you book.** Cover starts the day you buy the policy, so every week of delay is a week without protection. Many insurers also impose a deadline — often a few days after the first payment — to accept the full range of reasons.

## Cancellation-only or full travel policy?

- **Cancellation only**: if you already have medical cover for the trip (for example through an annual policy).
- **Full travel insurance with cancellation**: the simplest choice for most trips — one policy, one assistance line, from booking to return.

Before buying, check whether your **credit card** already includes cancellation cover and under what conditions. Our [credit card travel insurance guide](/guides/credit-card-travel-insurance) explains the usual limits.

## How to claim

1. Cancel with the airline, hotel or tour operator **as soon as** the reason arises — fees usually increase as departure approaches, and insurers only pay the fees due on the day the reason occurred.
2. Collect evidence: medical certificate, death certificate, employer letter, police report.
3. Send the invoices, the cancellation fees and the evidence to the insurer within the deadline in your conditions.
