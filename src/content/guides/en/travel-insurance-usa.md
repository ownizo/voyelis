The United States is one of the world's favourite destinations and, for medical care, by far one of the most expensive. A visit to the emergency room, a few nights in hospital or an ambulance ride can produce bills that would be unthinkable at home. Travel insurance is not legally required — but it is essential.

## Why the USA is different

- **Prices are set by each hospital** and are very high for people without US insurance.
- Hospitals may ask for **a guarantee of payment** before non-urgent treatment.
- Air ambulance and repatriation from North America are particularly costly.

That is why many policies charge more when the USA or Canada is among the destinations, and why some "worldwide" policies exclude them unless you select them.

## How much cover to choose

- **Medical expenses:** very high limits — ideally in the millions — or unlimited / actual cost.
- **Repatriation:** at actual cost.
- **Direct billing:** the insurer should pay the hospital directly.
- **Personal liability:** important in a country with high compensation awards, especially if you drive or rent sports equipment.

## What to check in the conditions

- **Pre-existing conditions:** if you have a chronic illness, check how it is handled. An undeclared condition can lead to refusal of the claim.
- **Age limits:** some policies increase prices or reduce cover above a certain age.
- **Activities:** skiing in Colorado, hiking in national parks or scuba diving may need an option.
- **Road trips:** the policy does not insure the rental car itself; check the rental contract for collision and liability cover.

## Credit card cover is rarely enough

Premium cards often include travel insurance, but medical limits and conditions vary widely and are not always designed for US prices. Read our [credit card travel insurance guide](/guides/credit-card-travel-insurance) before relying on it.

## If something happens

1. Call the **assistance number** on your policy before going to a hospital, unless it is a life-threatening emergency.
2. Keep every document: medical reports, invoices, prescriptions.
3. Do not sign payment commitments without speaking to the assistance company.
