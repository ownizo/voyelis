If you travel several times a year, buying a new policy each time quickly adds up — and it is easy to forget once. Annual multi-trip insurance covers every trip you take during twelve months with a single policy.

## How it works

- You are covered for **an unlimited number of trips** during the year.
- Each trip has a **maximum duration** — commonly between 30 and 90 days depending on the policy.
- The **territorial scope** is chosen at purchase: Europe only, or worldwide (sometimes with or without the USA and Canada).

## When it is worth it

As a rule of thumb, an annual policy becomes interesting from **two or three trips a year**, especially if:

- you travel for work and leisure;
- you take frequent short breaks;
- you travel outside Europe at least once a year;
- you want to be covered without thinking about it before each departure.

The easiest way to decide is to compare: on Voyelis, select **Annual multi-trip** and look at the price next to the cost of the single-trip policies you would otherwise buy.

## What to check

1. **Maximum trip length** — a sabbatical or long trip beyond it needs a separate policy.
2. **Worldwide or Europe** — and whether the USA is included.
3. **Medical limit and repatriation** — the same rules apply as for single trips: high limits, repatriation at actual cost.
4. **Cancellation** — included or not, and the limit per trip.
5. **Age limits** — some annual policies are only available up to a certain age.
6. **Family cover** — whether children travelling without you are also covered.

## Annual policy or credit card?

Premium credit cards often include a form of annual cover, but only when the trip is paid with the card and usually with lower limits. An annual policy covers you whatever the means of payment. See our [credit card guide](/guides/credit-card-travel-insurance).
